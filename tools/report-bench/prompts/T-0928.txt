- Keep the prose sections under roughly 350 words in total. They are read on a phone, standing up.
- One sentence per item. If an item needs two, the second is usually the part nobody will act on -- cut it.
- The Actions list is the exception: it is complete, however long that makes it.
- Never say the same thing twice, in one section or across two.
- Keep every figure, date and company name that was actually spoken; those are the parts a reader cannot reconstruct.

  provisional, say so alongside it.

  including anything a section describes as outside its own scope, handled
